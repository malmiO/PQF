Éclat: certified product photos and a promo video from your phone

Take your product photos. Éclat checks every one, keeps the best four and turns them into a short promo video.

Download for Android How it works

The problem small sellers have

Small online sellers need clean, consistent product photos to be taken seriously on marketplaces and social media, but most have no studio, no photographer and no time to learn editing. A blurry corner, a dim room or a cluttered background is easy to miss on a phone screen and costly once the listing is live, and making a promo video on top of that is another job entirely. Éclat checks each photo the moment you take it, tells you what to fix, and assembles the strongest set for you.

How it works

Each photo is scored by six technical checks and five AI presentation checks. The scores combine into one overall score. That score decides each photo's role, the best four become your set, and the set becomes a promo video.

See it work

Best setup

  • Light box or plain white background. A clean, even backdrop scores best on the background and colour checks.
  • Phone on a tripod or holder. A steady phone keeps every shot sharp and the angles repeatable.
  • Good, even light. Soft light from the sides or above; avoid harsh shadows and mixed colours.
  • One product, nothing else. Clear clutter from the frame.

  • Not sure? Check Settings, About phone. If Android says "App not installed" or "not compatible", try the other file.

Install on Android

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap a download button.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager (Settings, Apps, Special access, Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install, then open Éclat and allow camera access.
  4. When done, you can switch the "install unknown apps" permission off again.

Please note. Éclat is a research prototype, so it may not work properly on every phone. Phones differ a lot in their cameras and software.

Some phones apply their own automatic AI enhancement in the camera (such as AI scene or beauty modes, HDR or auto sharpening). This can make the live camera view freeze or get stuck, and it can also hide real problems from the checks. If that happens, turn the AI or scene enhancement off in your camera settings, then reopen the app.

The app is not signed by Google Play, so Android will warn you. Only install files from the buttons above.
